ncnn 20260526, android-vulkan arm64-v8a, built from the official full-source release
https://github.com/Tencent/ncnn/releases/download/20260526/ncnn-20260526-full-source.zip
(BSD-3-Clause) with NDK 27.2.12479018 and the release workflow's options, plus
-DNCNN_SIMPLEOMP=ON:

  cmake -DCMAKE_TOOLCHAIN_FILE=$NDK/build/cmake/android.toolchain.cmake \
    -DANDROID_ABI=arm64-v8a -DANDROID_PLATFORM=android-21 \
    -DANDROID_USE_LEGACY_TOOLCHAIN_FILE=False -DANDROID_SUPPORT_FLEXIBLE_PAGE_SIZES=ON \
    -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DNCNN_VULKAN=ON -DNCNN_SHARED_LIB=OFF \
    -DNCNN_BUILD_BENCHMARK=OFF -DNCNN_BUILD_TOOLS=OFF -DNCNN_BUILD_EXAMPLES=OFF \
    -DNCNN_SIMPLEOMP=ON ..
  cmake --build . -j4 && cmake --build . --target install/strip

NCNN_SIMPLEOMP: ncnn's own minimal OpenMP runtime instead of LLVM libomp. libomp's
CPU-affinity start-up aborted the app on Android 17 / Galaxy S25 Ultra
(__kmp_affinity_initialize -> __kmp_debug_assert) and no environment setting avoided
it; with simpleomp, libomp is not linked at all.
